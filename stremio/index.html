Before you install

The questions people ask most.

Which app should I use?

Use whichever sideloading app you already have. If you're starting fresh, SideStore and AltStore are the most common choices. Feather, ESign, KSign and GBox are for people who sign apps with their own certificate.

The source won't add. What now?

Each app reads a different format, so make sure the app you picked above matches the one you're adding it to. If the one-tap button does nothing, you're probably not on your iPhone — copy the URL and paste it into the app's Sources screen instead.

How do updates work?

Once the source is added, your app checks it for new versions. When Stremio updates, it appears in the app's updates list, with no need to come back here.

Is this official?

No. This is an independent source list that points to Stremio's own iOS download. It isn't made by or affiliated with Stremio.
